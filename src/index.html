Our Mission

Readers' Haven Public Library exists to inspire curiosity, support students and researchers, and provide free and affordable access to knowledge for every resident of our city. Whether you are looking for classic literature, academic references, or a quiet desk with high-speed Wi-Fi, our doors are open to you.
