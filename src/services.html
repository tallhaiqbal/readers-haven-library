Digital & On-Site Services

Readers' Haven offers modern digital tools and study facilities to all registered members.

1. Online Catalogue (OPAC)

Search real-time book availability, reserve titles, and renew borrowed items from anywhere.

Availability: 24/7 via library member portal • How to use: Sign in with your library card number.

2. E-Books & Audiobooks

Access 8,000+ licensed e-books, academic journals, and audiobooks on your phone, tablet, or laptop.

Loan Period: 14 days (auto-expires, zero late fees) • Limit: 3 concurrent downloads.

3. Study-Room Booking

Reserve quiet individual pods or 6-person group study rooms equipped with whiteboards and power outlets.

Timings: 9:30 AM – 7:30 PM (2-hour slots) • Booking: Reserve at the front desk or 24 hours ahead.

4. Printing, Scanning & Photocopy

Self-service B&W and color printing plus flatbed document scanning to USB or email.

Rates: PKR 5/page (B&W), PKR 25/page (Color), Scanning Free • Desk Hours: 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM.

5. Free High-Speed Wi-Fi

Complimentary fiber Wi-Fi throughout all reading halls, the children's wing, and study rooms.

Network: ReadersHaven-Guest • Access: Daily voucher issued at reception or via member login.
