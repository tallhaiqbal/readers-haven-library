Library Membership Plans

Choose an annual membership plan to borrow books, access reading rooms, and use digital library resources.

Student

PKR 500 / yr

  • Borrowing Limit: 3 books at a time
  • Loan Period: 14 days (1 renewal allowed)
  • Late-Return Rule: PKR 20 per book per day
  • Eligibility: Valid student ID required

Adult

PKR 1,500 / yr

  • Borrowing Limit: 6 books at a time
  • Loan Period: 21 days (up to 2 renewals)
  • Late-Return Rule: PKR 30 per book per day
  • Extras: Full access to e-books & quiet study pods

Family

PKR 3,000 / yr

  • Borrowing Limit: 12 books at a time (up to 4 cards)
  • Loan Period: 28 days (up to 2 renewals)
  • Late-Return Rule: PKR 25 per book per day
  • Extras: Children's story-hour priority & digital archive access

General Borrowing & Late-Return Rules

  • Renewals must be requested before the due date and are not available if another member has reserved the title.
  • Borrowing privileges are paused if unpaid late fees exceed PKR 500 or an item is overdue by more than 14 days.
  • Lost or damaged books must be replaced with the same edition or reimbursed at the library's current replacement cost.
